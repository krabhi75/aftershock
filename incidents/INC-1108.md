---
id: INC-1108
title: A naive clock marked an on-time parcel late
fault_class: naive_deadline
fixed_symbol: harborline.clocks.hub_intake_deadline
---

A parcel scanned at 18:10 in a hub seven hours behind UTC was compared with `datetime.now()` and no timezone. The naive clock treated the instant as late while the parcel was still inside its promise window.

`hub_intake_deadline` now keeps the instant in UTC. Linehaul departure, the customer promise, and hold expiry still call `datetime.now()` with no timezone argument, so the same comparison can fire on those clocks.
